## Where to start

New to Bagisto? Read the [Introduction](./getting-started/introduction.md), learn your way around the [Admin Panel](./getting-started/admin-panel.md), explore the [Point of Sale (POS)](./plugins/pos.md) and extensions, then follow the [Store Setup Checklist](./getting-started/store-setup-checklist.md) from a fresh install to your first order.

## How this guide is organized

The guide follows the operational rhythm of running a store: **Getting Started**, **Extensions & Plugins (POS, Landing Pages, Purchases, WhatsApp)**, **Catalog & Products**, **Sales & Orders**, **Customers**, **Marketing & Pixels**, **Appearance**, and **Comprehensive System Configuration**.
