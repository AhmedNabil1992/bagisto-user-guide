# Order Confirmation, WhatsApp & Fraud Prevention

The Order Confirmation and Fraud Blacklist suite is the vital operational control room for cash-on-delivery (COD) merchants. It equips call centers and fulfillment teams with rapid order verification, automated WhatsApp customer notifications, and instant fraudulent customer blocking to slash return-to-origin (RTO) waste.

---

## 1. Call Center Confirmation Dashboard

Access **Order Confirm** from the main admin navigation:
- **Dedicated Operational View**: View unconfirmed orders with essential buyer info: Customer Name, Phone, Address, Total, and Order Time.
- **Single-Click Status Updates**:
  - **Confirmed**: Order verified and ready for fulfillment.
  - **Pending**: Awaiting initial customer verification call.
  - **No Answer**: Customer did not pick up; scheduled for automatic callback.
  - **Cancelled**: Order cancelled upon customer request.
  - **Duplicate**: Merged or duplicate order.
- **Internal Call Notes**: Log interaction notes, delivery instructions, or callback arrangements.

---

## 2. Automated WhatsApp Integration (Evolution API)

Send instant conversational notifications directly to the customer's WhatsApp:
- **Direct WhatsApp Connection**: Connect your business WhatsApp number using Evolution API via simple QR-code scan.
- **Instant Order Confirmation Messages**: Automatically ping buyers with their order summary, items, and verification prompts upon checkout.
- **Tracking & Dispatch Updates**: Send courier tracking numbers and out-for-delivery alerts automatically to maximize delivery success rates.

---

## 3. Fraud Blacklist & Fake Order Protection

Shield your business from repeat defaulters and competitive harassment:
- **One-Click Quick Block**: Instantly add fraudulent phone numbers or abusive customers to the Blacklist directly from the confirmation screen.
- **Automated Order Blocking**: Blacklisted phone numbers are flagged or blocked from placing future orders, saving courier return penalties.
- **Blacklist Management**: Maintain reason codes, historical block logs, and unblock accounts when required.
