# Local Payment Gateways & Shipping Couriers

Bagisto integrates comprehensive support for regional payment gateways and domestic shipping carriers tailored for Egypt and Middle East commerce. Enable seamless mobile wallet payments, instant bank transfers, card processing, and automated courier order booking directly from your admin panel.

---

## 1. Supported Payment Gateways

Accept payments securely with low transaction fees and instant confirmation:

### Mobile Wallets & Instant Banking
- **Vodafone Cash & Digital Wallets**: Collect payments through Egypt's leading mobile wallets (Vodafone Cash, Orange Cash, Etisalat Cash, WE Pay). Customers can send the amount to your wallet number and optionally upload receipt proof during checkout.
- **InstaPay (IPN)**: Accept instant domestic bank-to-bank transfers via the Central Bank of Egypt's Instant Payment Network (InstaPay) using your registered IPA address, phone number, or account IBAN.

### Online Card & Multi-Channel Gateways
- **Paymob**: Accept Visa, Mastercard, Meeza cards, mobile wallets, and installments (ValU, Sympl, Souhoola) with automated webhook callbacks and transaction reconciliation.
- **Kashier**: Quick and reliable online debit/credit card processing, Meeza support, and hosted checkout flow.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Configure regional COD fees, minimum/maximum order totals, and restrict COD to verified customer groups or geographical zones.

---

## 2. Integrated Shipping Couriers

Connect directly with regional parcel carriers to print shipping labels, track packages, and book pickups:

- **Bosta**: Complete integration with Bosta courier API for real-time tracking, automatic airway bill (AWB) generation, and webhook updates on delivery status.
- **Aramex**: Global and domestic shipping, rate calculation, pickup scheduling, and international parcel customs manifests.
- **Mylerz**: Next-day and same-day urban parcel delivery with COD collection management and package returns handling.
- **J&T Express**: High-volume eCommerce logistics with automated label generation and status synchronization.

---

## 3. Configuration & Rates

- **Zone-Based Pricing**: Set flat-rate or weight-tiered shipping fees by governorate / city (e.g., Cairo & Giza, Alexandria, Delta, Upper Egypt).
- **Free Shipping Thresholds**: Offer automatic free shipping when the cart total exceeds a configurable order value.
- **Live Courier Tracking**: Display real-time tracking links directly in the customer account area and order confirmation emails.
