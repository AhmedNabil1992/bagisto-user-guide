# Purchases & Suppliers

The Purchases module delivers a complete enterprise supply-chain workflow directly inside Bagisto: manage vendors and suppliers, issue purchase orders/invoices, receive incoming warehouse shipments with automated inventory updates, and record supplier disbursements and cost analytics.

---

## 1. Supplier Management

Navigate to **Purchases >> Suppliers**:
- **Add Suppliers**: Record company name, contact representative, email, phone number, VAT numbers, and physical address.
- **Vendor Ledger**: Review outstanding supplier balances, total invoiced values, and historical settlement records.

---

## 2. Purchase Invoices & Stock Intake

1. Go to **Purchases >> Invoices**.
2. Click **Create Invoice**.
3. Select the **Supplier** and the target **Inventory Source (Warehouse)**.
4. Search and append catalog items to the invoice, defining:
   - Quantities ordered.
   - Unit wholesale purchase cost.
   - Associated taxes, import shipping fees, or trade discounts.
5. Save the invoice.

### Receiving Warehouse Shipments
When delivery manifests arrive at your receiving dock:
- Open the purchase invoice and click **Receive Products**.
- Bagisto automatically credits the physical inventory counts in the designated inventory source and recalculates product unit cost baselines.

---

## 3. Supplier Payments

From the purchase invoice detail or payments view:
- Record outgoing vendor payments (Bank Wire, Cash, Cheque).
- Real-time invoice balance tracking with dynamic status transitions: Unpaid, Partially Paid, and Fully Paid.

---

## 4. Cost & Purchasing Reports

Navigate to **Reporting >> Purchases**:
- Aggregate expenditure and total Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) over custom date windows.
- Supplier concentration and most frequently purchased catalog items.
- Profit margin analytics evaluating purchase cost variance against retail sale prices.
