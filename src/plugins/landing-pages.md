# Single Product Landing Funnels

The Single Product Landing Funnels module is engineered specifically for media buyers, growth marketers, and direct-response campaigns. It converts standard Bagisto catalog products into distraction-free, high-converting dedicated sales funnels.

---

## 1. Why Use a Product Landing Funnel?

Standard product detail pages often overwhelm buyers with navigation links, unrelated categories, and complex multi-step checkout processes. A landing funnel presents a single, laser-focused narrative emphasizing product benefits, social proof, and a streamlined 1-click order form.

---

## 2. Creating a Funnel

1. In the admin sidebar, navigate to **Catalog >> Landing Pages (Funnels)**.
2. Select the product you want to promote from the top dropdown list.
3. Click **Create Landing Funnel**.
4. Toggle **Status** to **Active** to publish the dedicated funnel view.

---

## 3. Funnel Customization Options

- **Compelling Headline**: A bold, benefit-driven hook at the top of the viewport (e.g., "The Ultimate Ergonomic Cushion for All-Day Comfort").
- **Accent Color**: Choose a brand accent color applied to call-to-action buttons, badges, and focal points.
- **Engaging Media**: Embed explanatory product videos and high-resolution lifestyle images.
- **Key Features & Benefits**: Visually structured benefit bullets with custom icons addressing customer pain points.
- **Customer Reviews & Social Proof**: Curated photo reviews and ratings that build instant buyer trust.
- **FAQ Accordion**: Pre-empt common buyer objections regarding warranty, delivery speed, and return policies.

---

## 4. Integrated 1-Click Order Form

Each landing page embeds an optimized direct-order form:
- Requires only essential fields: Name, Phone Number, and Shipping Address.
- Option/variant selector for size, color, and package tiers directly inside the form.
- Quantity upsell bundles (e.g., "Buy 2 Get Free Shipping").
- Automatically creates standard Bagisto orders in the admin panel with support for Cash on Delivery and online gateways.
