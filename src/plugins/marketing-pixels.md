# Marketing Pixels & Meta Catalog

The Marketing Pixels suite links your storefront directly with major ad tech networks to gather precise conversion events, train algorithmic bid models, and automate product feeds for dynamic retargeting campaigns.

---

## 1. Supported Ad Networks

Configure pixel identifiers without touching template code:
- **Meta Pixel (Facebook & Instagram)**: Native server-side and browser tracking for Meta Advantage+ campaigns.
- **TikTok Pixel**: Complete conversion measurement for TikTok Ads Manager.
- **Snapchat Pixel**: Funnel event tracking across Snapchat campaigns.
- **Google Tag Manager & Google Analytics 4 (GA4)**: Enterprise tag container integration.

---

## 2. Tracked eCommerce Events

Bagisto automatically triggers high-fidelity standard events:
- `PageView`: General page visits.
- `ViewContent`: Product detail views with SKU, category, and pricing.
- `AddToCart`: Add-to-cart events with product IDs and basket values.
- `InitiateCheckout`: Checkout screen entry.
- `Purchase`: Confirmed order conversions passing transaction value, currency, order ID, and purchased line items.

---

## 3. Pixel Event Logs & Live Testing

Under **Marketing Pixels >> Logs**:
- **Event Audit Logs**: Inspect raw event payloads, timestamps, and delivery responses to ensure zero drop-off.
- **Test Send Trigger**: Send test conversion events with a single click to verify connectivity in Meta Events Manager or TikTok Test Events.

---

## 4. Meta XML Catalog Feed

- Generates a live, self-updating XML product feed fully compliant with Meta Commerce Manager specifications.
- Syncs prices, descriptions, images, and inventory automatically so Facebook & Instagram catalog shops stay updated in real time.
