# Back in Stock Notifications

The Back in Stock notification module allows customers and visitors to subscribe to automated restock alerts on out-of-stock products. Recover potential lost sales by instantly notifying interested shoppers the moment fresh inventory is added to your warehouse.

---

## 1. Storefront Experience

When a product inventory drops to zero:
- **"Notify Me When Available" Button**: The standard "Add to Cart" button is automatically replaced by an elegant restock subscription button.
- **Guest & Customer Subscription**:
  - Registered logged-in customers can subscribe with a single click using their account email address.
  - Guest shoppers simply enter their email address in a clean popup prompt to join the notification queue.
- **Variant Support**: For configurable products (e.g. specific sizes or colors that are out of stock), customers can subscribe specifically to the individual variant they want.

---

## 2. Managing Subscriptions in Admin

Track demand and prioritize your restocking schedules:
- **Subscription List**: View all active restock requests organized by product name, SKU, customer email, and subscription date.
- **High-Demand Insights**: Identify which out-of-stock items have the highest number of pending subscriber requests to guide your purchasing decisions.
- **Status Tracking**: Monitor whether an alert is `Pending` or has already been `Sent`.

---

## 3. Automated Restock Dispatch

- **Automatic Trigger**: When an admin or inventory manager updates product stock quantities (via admin edit, bulk CSV import, or purchase order receiving), the system automatically detects newly available items.
- **Instant Email Alerts**: Pre-designed, branded email templates are dispatched to all waiting subscribers informing them that their desired product is back in stock with a direct link to purchase.
- **Queue Throttling**: Efficient background queuing ensures high volumes of notification emails are sent smoothly without impacting storefront speed.
