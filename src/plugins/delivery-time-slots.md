# Delivery Time Slots

The Delivery Time Slots extension empowers shoppers to select their preferred delivery date and convenience time window during checkout. This streamlines fulfillment logistics, prevents failed delivery attempts, and significantly cuts return-to-origin (RTO) shipping fees.

---

## 1. Setting Up Delivery Slots

Navigate to **Delivery Time Slot** in the admin sidebar:
- **Configure Windows**: Specify delivery days (Monday through Sunday) with start and end times (e.g., 10:00 AM – 02:00 PM or 05:00 PM – 09:00 PM).
- **Slot Capacity Threshold**: Define the maximum quota of orders your courier team can handle per slot. Once capacity is reached, the slot automatically closes to avoid shipping bottlenecks.
- **Excluded Days & Holidays**: Blackout specific calendar dates, weekends, or national holidays from the scheduling picker.

---

## 2. Storefront Checkout Flow

- During shipping selection at checkout, shoppers see an interactive date and time picker.
- Selecting a delivery date reveals all available time slots while graying out booked or unavailable windows.
- The confirmed slot is attached to the customer order record, invoice, and packing slip.

---

## 3. Managing Delivery Orders

Under **Delivery Time Slot >> Delivery Orders**:
- Filter pending dispatches by target fulfillment date and delivery window.
- Organize courier routes and packing manifests grouped by morning, afternoon, or evening batches.
