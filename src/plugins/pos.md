# Point of Sale (POS)

Bagisto's integrated Point of Sale (POS) system connects your brick-and-mortar stores, pop-up shops, and physical outlets directly with your online eCommerce platform. Enjoy real-time inventory synchronization, shift reconciliation, thermal receipt printing, and comprehensive cashbox tracking.

---

## 1. POS Terminal

The intuitive, high-speed cashier checkout interface:
- **Product & Barcode Search**: Scan product barcodes directly with any standard barcode scanner to instantly add items to the cart, or search by name and SKU.
- **Cart & Discounts**: Adjust quantities, apply on-the-spot cart discounts, and configure options such as size and color.
- **Customer Selection**: Choose the default **Walk-in Customer (Cash)** with a single click, search for existing registered customers, or quickly register a new customer without leaving the terminal.
- **Multiple Payment Methods**: Accept **Cash**, **Card / POS Terminal**, or split payments across multiple methods.
- **Receipt Printing**: Generate and print standardized thermal receipts compatible with 80mm and 58mm thermal POS printers.

---

## 2. Cashier Shifts & Sessions

Monitor cashier cash flow and shift balances with zero discrepancies:
- **Open Session**: Cashiers begin their shift by entering the **Opening Cash Balance** in the till.
- **Live Sales Tracking**: Real-time aggregation of cash sales, card sales, and total transactions during the shift.
- **Close Session**: At the end of the shift, the cashier enters the counted **Closing Cash**.
- **Difference & Discrepancy Reporting**: The system automatically compares expected drawer cash against actual cash, highlighting any surplus or shortage alongside timestamps and cashier identifiers.

---

## 3. Outlets & Branches

Manage multiple retail locations:
- **Configure Outlets**: Set branch names, physical addresses, and contact details.
- **Inventory Source Mapping**: Link each outlet to a dedicated Bagisto **Inventory Source**. Sales made at a specific outlet deduct inventory exclusively from its assigned warehouse.

---

## 4. Cashboxes & Treasuries

Complete control over physical cash movements:
- **Cashbox Setup**: Create master treasuries and branch cashboxes.
- **Cash Transactions**: Record deposits, petty cash expenses, and till top-ups with reasons and references.
- **Cash Transfers**: Safely transfer funds between cash registers and the central company treasury with audit logs.
